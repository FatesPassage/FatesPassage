## The Three Sun Gods — canon
The three suns are the ascended first Triunes: **Dronnar**, **Oscrom**, and **Melarmony**. In the earliest age, three royal Fatewalkers of the first civilization were revered for their skill and prowess in battle. They were the first to discover **the Element**; its power changed them into the original three Triunes. They used their strength to keep peace.

When the suns began to burn out, the three ascended beyond their former scale and became the three suns in Arkaedon's sky, preserving the land and its people. Their story has been passed down for millennia. Their names, identities, and great sacrifice remain common knowledge, while the precise history of the first civilization and the full nature of the Element have faded or been lost. Do not automatically equate this ancient Element with the later Core element; that relationship remains unresolved.

- **Dronnar — The Crimson Bulwark:** largest red sun. A formidable, imposing royal with a menacing presence, but not evil; his defining currents are **Valor, Resolve, and Guardianship**. His strength exists to protect.
- **Oscrom — The Golden Arbiter:** middle golden sun. The wise, measured royal whose golden demeanor brought counsel and balance; his defining currents are **Wisdom, Balance, and Foresight**.
- **Melarmony — The White Benediction:** smallest pearly-white sun. The bright, holy, delicate healer whose compassion and courage sustained others; her defining currents are **Mercy, Renewal, and Grace**.

The public legend should be referenced naturally throughout Arkaedon's story, dialogue, rituals, sayings, festivals, and landmarks. Deeper historical detail can be revealed through ruins, relics, and research. The gods' present consciousness or ability to communicate is not yet established. This canon replaces only the guide's former unnamed-suns default in §30.8(F); it does not rewrite the separate meteor or Ashfall events or their chronology.
